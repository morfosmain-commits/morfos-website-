House rules

Section 04
Rule one

We do not ship what we would not stake our name on

The rebuild-until-right guarantee is the rule written down, not a slogan bolted onto it. If it is not right, it is not finished, and the clock is ours.

That is only possible because capacity is capped at 12 builds a month. The guarantee holds because the workload does.

Rule two

Every client is founder-accountable

You get a named founder on your project. Not an account manager, not a shared inbox, and not a ticket number.

We stay reachable until you do not need us. That is built directly against the thing this category fails at most: the vendor who goes quiet.

Rule three

Words you will not find on this site

Plain language is a policy here, not a style preference. These are struck from every page, deck and email we send.

Affordable · seamless · cutting-edge · scalable · end-to-end · revolutionary · world-class · game-changing · synergy · streamlined · holistic · best-in-class · innovative
